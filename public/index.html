About Realworld Labs

Explore our product ecosystem

We build the hardware and software that connect embodied intelligence with physical action.

Our work spans dexterous hands, robotic arms, and an embodied AI software platform. We bring these capabilities into one product ecosystem, helping researchers and developers move between physical interaction, data collection, model training, and deployment.

01 / Physical interaction

Start with the real world.

RealHand dexterous hands and the RealArm P7 provide the hardware for manipulation, motion, and interaction with the world.

02 / Connected development

Bring the workflow together.

RealStudio brings Control, Collect, Train, and Deploy into one platform, supporting a connected workflow from robot operation to on-site deployment.

03 / What comes next

Build toward a fuller ecosystem.

Our roadmap includes RealBot humanoid and quadruped robots and RealBrain, our core model. Both product families are coming soon.
