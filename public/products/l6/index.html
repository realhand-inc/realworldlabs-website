Dexterous Hand

L6

Compact by design. Precise in motion.

A compact, power-efficient dexterous hand with 6 active and 5 passive degrees of freedom, designed for embedded systems and scaled deployments.

Discuss L6Technical specifications

Designed for the task

Explore L6.

01

Compact integration

A 618.5 g hand with a 24 V DC power supply, CAN 2.0 communication, and an ISO 9409-1-50-4-M6 wrist flange.

02

Repeatable motion

Active joints operate at 180 deg/s, with a 0.35-second opening/closing response and +/-0.2 mm repeat positioning accuracy.

03

Mechanically coupled grasping

Passive joints follow their corresponding active joints through mechanical coupling, supporting natural grasping motions.
