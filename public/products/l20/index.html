Dexterous Hand

L20

More articulation. Stronger grasping.

A high-DOF dexterous hand with 16 active and 5 passive joints, 100 N five-finger grip force, and tactile sensing options for advanced manipulation.

Designed for the task

Explore L20.

01

Articulated manipulation

21 joints combine 16 active and 5 passive joints. Four-finger abduction provides +/-10 degrees of side swing.

02

Force and repeatability

100 N five-finger grip force, a 10 kg maximum load, and +/-0.2 mm repeat positioning accuracy.

03

Tactile sensing options

A 6x12 pressure array, with optional capacitive 3D force, camera, and visual-tactile sensing.
